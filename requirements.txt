# zarr 3. The pin used to read "2.x on purpose", on the grounds that zarr 3
# changed append_dim and region-write semantics; that was wrong and the
# migration measured it. append_dim appends (leads come back [6, 12, 18], values
# intact) and region writes rebuilt the whole 50 GB archive elementwise-identical
# to the v2 store. What did change is the codec spelling — v2's single
# `compressor` became a `compressors` list — and the default, from Blosc-lz4 to
# Zstd without shuffle, which is why postprocess names its codec explicitly now.
